# Personal Portfolio Website

**Project 02 | Website Creation & Digital Presence**

## Project Overview

A personal portfolio website created using Google Sites to establish a professional online presence, introduce my background and services, showcase projects and provide accessible ways for people to get in touch.

## Project Objective

To create a professional, accessible and functional personal website that brings together professional information, services, portfolio projects and contact options in one place.

The website was created using a free website-building platform, demonstrating a practical and budget-friendly approach to establishing an online presence.

## Platform & Tools

- Google Sites
- Google Forms
- Google Sheets

## Website Structure

- **Home** – Professional introduction, overview of services and how I work.
- **About Me** – Background, approach, values and what people can expect when working with me.
- **How I Can Help** – Overview of services and areas of support.
- **Portfolio** – Projects, practical solutions and links to GitHub and LinkedIn.
- **Get in Touch** – Location, availability, contact details, online profiles and enquiry form.

## Key Features

- Professional personal website and online presence
- Clear presentation of services and areas of support
- Dedicated portfolio page linking to professional profiles and projects
- Contact options including email and WhatsApp
- Integrated enquiry form for incoming enquiries
- Custom favicon and website branding
- Mobile presentation checked as part of a visual quality check
